SPIRITWEAVER

INTUITION • ADAPTABILITY • SPEECH
The Weave. The everlasting, undying weave. The essence of magic condensed into locks of hair braided together. The Great Spiritweaver undying and forever enshrined in their tomb in the Great Hall of the Weave. Locks of their hair given to their pupils and their pupils' pupils and their pupils' pupils' pupils... The lesson of the Weave passing ever onwards. These monks turned spellcasters utilize their Weave to harness the essence of the Weave and cast with it in contact with their skin. Masters at martial arts, the Spiritweavers entwine their attacks with magic in the form of imbuements or essence manipulation. Will you carry the mantle of the Weave and abide to laws of the Weavers?

Class Traits

The Connection Enhancement
In combat, a Spiritweaver makes a constant connection to the Great Spiritweaver with the Weave in hand. Combat is enhanced. The ability to do more, see more, and act upon it all. The mystic ways of the monks of old beget faster methods of fighting.
Threshold for multiple actions is reduced by 5 (i.e., from Agility 1-5 you have 2 actions, instead of 1 action)
Weaver's Imbuement Enhancement
With the Weave in hand, a Spiritweaver can quickly cast magic while fighting with their other hand. Every action has the potential to be explosive with magic. Multiple imbuements aligning together to provide major damage.
Typically, spells require a full action to cast, but with a Spiritweaver you can cast an imbuement and ensue in combat with each action
A Weaver's Folly Adjustment
Weaver's train with the magic of imbuement, but can learn other magic as well. Spiritweavers utilize their other magic as catch all of their power exhausting themselves with every cast.
If a non-imbuement combat spell is used, all actions are taken. Multiply the damage by each action taken.
