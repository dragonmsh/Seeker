THIEF

FINESSE • INTUITION • STEALTH
Hiding in the shadows, the Thief lurks looking for its next prey. Prey being a sorry sop with loads of cash or jewelry walking home at night. The Thief strikes quickly and quietly using the shadows to their advantage. The sounds of cats paws on roof tiles or the rip-rap of rain on soggy soil, the Thief is unheard. Blending with urban environments and disguising themselves with their unique foresight, the Thief is no match when perusing for fine goods at a 100% discount. Are you sly?

Class Traits

In the Shadows Advantage
Thieves have enhanced stealth in the shadows. They creep and crawl like a wily leopard stalking its prey. The Thief becomes one with the shadows and can eavesdrop, commit thievery, and kill in almost plainsight.
Gain advantage to Stealth checks
Stalker Enhancement
As stealthy as they are, they are just as quiet. They can creep in the shadows monitoring the exact moment that a strike would be critical. They can use items as quiet as a gentle breeze. The leaves flutter, but the Thief does not.
While stealthed, you deal double damage
Magicless Negative
Thieves don't use magic. They see little use in it in their craft. Magic is noisy and bright, the opposite of a Thief.
Arcana loses 4 to modifier
