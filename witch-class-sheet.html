WITCH

ARCANE • ADAPTABILITY • ARCANA
Masters of hexes, vexes, and curses, the Witches are unique natural spellcasters. They prefer to use component or glyph based magic that imposes some form of difficulty or harm on their subjects. Many Witches don't even need to directly contact their subject to place a curse on them, they merely need to know who it is. Preferring dark magic, Witches (male or female) typically harm their subjects by affecting their psyche. They can make them see things that aren't really there or give them intense pain or emotions. Curses or hexes or vexes, may they be your tricks?

Class Traits

Accursed Touch Enhancement
Witches are well-versed with hexes, vexes, and curses. But most importantly, those curses a Witch can cast can be casted with a single touch. Witches don't need any verbal or visual components to cast their spells.
Witch catalysts are purely for enhancement. Witches can cast without a catalyst. Hexes, vexes, and curses have double the duration and one single one can be active simultaneously on multiple targets.
Darkness Combat
Witches dabble in dark magic. Everything they do involves some form of dark magic and is enhanced by the darkness.
All combat spells add Dark DR equivalent to Class Skill level (i.e. CS0 → 0 Dark DR, CS10 → 10 Dark DR)
Light Weakness Adjustment
A Witches inherent weakness is light magic. It weakens them, making their magic significantly weaker. Darkness cannot grow with the intensity of the light from many spells.
Take double damage from light magic. Spell DR is reduced by half.
