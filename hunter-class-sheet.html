HUNTER

FINESSE • INTELLIGENCE • SURVIVAL
Hunters typically from villages and camps around the world. They have a role in society in not only finding and hunting food, but to also protect villages and camps from raids from bandits or orcs clans. They are uniquely adapted to the terrain they are from and can quickly trek many miles without exhaustion. They tend to use ranged weapons and typically work alone and tend to keep to themselves when they do go to any city. Hunters may know some basic magic for first aid or for traps. Does being the wilds call to you?

Class Traits

Hidden Stalker Advantage
Hunters' are natural survivalists. They can survive off anything in the land. They tend to not rely on others, so this makes survival significantly easier for them. If with others, they can use their survival skills to create a stable and adequate camping site for all and will know how to actively survive without rations.
Gain advantage to Survival checks
Skilled Trapper Ability
Hunter's are skilled trappers and can make traps out of anything they find while out on hunt. All they need is some twine or rope and a trap can be made.
Gain Trap Craft ability with infinite charges
Silent Disadvantage
Hunters don't talk much, but when they do they typically don't have the best manners or say the right things. They are quite socially awkward and tend to make some situations worse than they should be.
Gain disadvantage to Speech checks
