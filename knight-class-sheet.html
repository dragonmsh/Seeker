KNIGHT

STRENGTH • FINESSE • MIGHT
Mighty and strong, the Knights throughout Estera are a different breed than the standard guard or soldier. They are skilled fighters who choose the gear that benefits them the most. Their armor, their weapons, and their tools are carefully selected for their combat style of choice. Knights do not use magic to uphold their oath to standard combat. Knights travel high and low for new work and will take most of their opportunities at job boards to gain more renown. Is chivalry your calling?

Class Traits

Mightiest Favor Advantage
Knights are not inherently valorant, but if you are in their favor or getting paid by you, they will use their might to oppose all against you.
Gain advantage to Might checks
Valiant Blade Ability
Knights are well skilled with blade weapons. They can control a well honed blade with valiance and triumph over their opponents.
Gain Valiant Slash ability with infinite charges
Heavy-Footed Negative
Knights aren't suitable for sneaking around. Even with lighter clothing, they cannot walk quietly and will typically be stomping around. Silence isn't their best ability.
Stealth loses 4 to modifier
