ORACLE

ARCANE • INTUITION • RESISTANCE
The ones who see the light. Naturalborn healers, the Oracles are divine by birthright. Bestowed powers beyond mortal means. Infused with the Great Phoenix's light magic, the Oracles grant all they touch with immense life; healing wounds no Curator or Witch-doctor ever could. Oracles' divine light provides sanctuary to those in need. The gods do not show themselves or speak to the Oracles, their power is bestowed and they vanish like they always have. Oracles tend to travel to bestow their light to all who need it, but many do situate themselves and form sanctuaries or congregations to provide stable healing houses. May it be you who is bestowed with the light?

Class Traits

Raise the Fallen Spell
Oracles are lighting the way. Phoenix's blessing reaches to the beyond. The ever swallowing and destroying beyond. One soul can be saved with the Phoenix's light. But be prepared, the cost is high.
Gain the Phoenix's Light spell with unlimited casts
The Phoenix's Will Advantage
Oracles carry the will of the Phoenix with them. Enhanced by the gods and touched by the Great Phoenix, Oracles resound with willpower that many others fail to grasp. They stand against any adversity to provide the light to all.
Gain advantage to Willpower checks
Flip-Flop Negative
They may be traveling light givers, but they tend to keep to the roads and avoid scenic detours that involve jumping or climbing sheer cliffs. Oracles are not the most acclimated to intense body activity and rely on others to assist them to get around.
Athletics loses 4 to modifier
