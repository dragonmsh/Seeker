CASTER

ARCANE • INTUITION • BENDING
One with ways of magic. You are able to bend and wield magic typically with a catalyst of some form. You bend the elements. You control the charged electric energy in the environment and wield it against your foes. Or you may utilize your magic to aid those around you. The choice is yours.

Class Traits

Bending Magic Modification
Casters are natural element benders. They can utilize the four key elements (fire, earth, wind, and water) to produce intricate magical chains with such elements. They can take earth and make it into a pillar. They can combust the air in front them. They can control the wind and push others away. And they can create whips of water.
Bending gains +4 to modifier
Magical Perception Spell
Casters can see the magic in the air. They can feel the charged energy and grasp at it. They can see objects magically attuned without a single cast. If there is magical, they will both see it and feel it before any other.
Gain Magic Sense spell with infinite casts
Indefensible Negative
Casters are a little wild. They tend to not heed warnings and dive in head first. They believe their magical prowess is beyond the control of others, but one good smack and they're usually down. Their defensive capabilities are greatly reduced.
Defense loses -4 to modifier
