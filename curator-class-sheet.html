CURATOR

INTELLIGENCE • STRENGTH • CRAFT
Well-studied in the ways of healing magic from one of the many academies around the world. You go amongst the land, amongs the people, and most especially amongst the weak and injured. You heal. You strengthen. You give resolve to those that see no hope. You give them a reason to continue. You will do nothing ubt fight for the common good and well-being of the people of this world.

Class Traits

Healing Mantra SPELL
Curators' are taught healing magic very early into their academy years. They are separated from the Magi and given rigourous teaching to use everything, including their voice, to heal others.
Gain Healing Voice spell with infinite casts.
Medical Crafting ADVANTAGE
Curators are trained to produce medical devices or tools from items on hand or in the vicinity of an incident to provide medical care.
Gain advantage to Craft checks.
Unimaginative NEGATIVE
Curators are not known for their artistry skills. In fact, they rarely have time to do any art with their constant practice of medicine and healing magic.
Artistry loses 4 to modifier.
