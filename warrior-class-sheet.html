WARRIOR

STRENGTH • ADAPTABILITY • ATHLETICS
The nomadic warrior. Coming from tribes from all around the lands, the warrior is a fierce and brutal fighter. Typically wielding two weapons, the warrior fights with a unique style, utilizing both weapons as efficiently as possible. Many of the warriors are found in the more rural areas hunting for food and protecting their tribe. Many do travel to experience combat in various forms to hone their skills when going back to their tribe. Does the ferocity call to you?

Class Traits

Two-Weapon Fighting Combat
Warriors are masters at effective combat. Wielding two weapons in each hand, they can efficiently use both weapons at the same time. Typically, warriors use two axes or two clubs, but they can adapt to using other weapons and maintain their two-weapon fighting.
If wielding two weapons, you can strike twice in one attack (rolling separate hits)
Sporty Modification
Warrior tribes enforce strength and flexibility. They play many sports in their youth to enhance future warriors with the skills necessary to learn combat. Many of these sports or games instill greater athletics involving jumping, running, throwing, and climbing.
Athletics gains +4 to modifier
Inhibited Arcana Disadvantage
Warriors train their bodies and their skills, but one skill they do not hone is in Arcana. They don't believe seeing or feeling magic is important. They believe we must see and feel what is truly physically there, not what is not.
Gain disadvantage to Arcana
